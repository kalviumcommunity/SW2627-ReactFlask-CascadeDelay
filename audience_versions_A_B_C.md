# Audience-Specific Versions

## Version A — Board of Directors

Customer churn is currently 7% and represents approximately $2M in annual revenue loss. Support delays are a significant retention risk: churn is 3% when support is provided within 2 hours compared with 12% when customers wait more than 24 hours. High-value customers are particularly vulnerable, with 15% churn when support is slow. The proposed $200K annual investment in two Support Engineers, together with a $50K engineering investment for high-value customer prioritization, targets a projected $400K reduction in churn-related losses during the first year. The requested investment is designed to protect revenue, reduce customer losses, and improve retention.

---

## Version B — Operations Team

The immediate operational priority is to reduce the current average support response time from 6 hours to below 2 hours. The first process change is to establish and track a less-than-2-hour response SLA beginning January 1. Support performance should be monitored daily so that delays can be identified and addressed quickly.

Two additional Support Engineers should be hired to increase support capacity and reduce response pressure on the existing team. A dedicated support lane for customers spending more than $10K annually should also be implemented by February 1. This routing change will prioritize the customers most sensitive to slow support. Operations should review the staffing and SLA plan by December 15, Finance should approve the budget by December 20, hiring should begin January 1, and new hires should become productive by April 1.

---

## Version C — Support Team

The goal of this initiative is to reduce the pressure on the existing support team while improving the customer experience. Current response time averages 6 hours, while customers who receive support within 2 hours have substantially lower churn. Hiring two additional Support Engineers will increase team capacity and help reduce workload pressure.

A less-than-2-hour response SLA will provide a clear shared target and help the team prioritize work. Customers spending more than $10K annually will receive priority routing through a dedicated support lane. The implementation plan starts with the SLA on January 1, followed by high-value customer routing on February 1. New team members are expected to become productive by April 1. The objective is to give the support team more capacity, clearer priorities, and better tools while improving customer retention.
